# Recommended 4–5 Minute Panel Demo

1. Show `/health`: saved ML model ready + AI guidance ready.
2. Show the candidate page and enter a target role.
3. Record or upload a fresh answer.
4. Explain the runtime pipeline while the request runs:
   - Faster-Whisper creates transcript.
   - NLP + audio features are extracted.
   - saved Linear SVM predicts the communication category.
   - deterministic evidence identifies observable answer properties.
   - OpenAI converts that evidence into personalised coaching without changing the category.
5. Show the result category and transcript.
6. Show "What you already demonstrate" and "What to build next".
7. Show one Professional English rewrite and one speaking pattern.
8. Play the practice answer with the Listen button.
9. Show the STAR coach and seven-day practice plan.
10. Complete one task and point out that the progress bar measures practice completion, not communication skill.
11. Ask the follow-up coach: "Give me a stronger opening for this answer."
12. Show Attempt History and explain the feedback loop for future recordings.

## One-sentence contribution

> The system does not stop at classifying communication as Poor, Average, Good, or Excellent; it converts explainable evidence from the candidate's own interview answer into grounded, personalised, trackable interview-speaking practice.
