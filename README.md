### About this research

Rapid change in technology and the labour market has widened the gap between formal education, employability readiness, and sustainable career development. This project investigates an **ethical, explainable, decision-support framework**—not an automated hiring system—that brings together multimodal signals and causal-style reasoning to support **interview readiness**, **behaviour-aware career improvement**, **long-term career safety**, and **transparent employer-side insights**.

The work aligns with **UN Sustainable Development Goals** [4 (Quality Education)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4), [8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal8), and [9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9), emphasising responsible and interpretable use of AI in education and recruitment contexts.

---

### Main objective

To bridge education and industry by improving how individuals assess career readiness and suitable paths, and how employers reason about candidate readiness—using **observable skills, behaviours, and career patterns** within a structured, human-in-the-loop assessment design.

### Conceptual focus (solution outline)

- **Interview & path guidance** — Interpretable preparation support aligned with realistic roles.  
- **Behaviour–outcome modelling** — Scenario-driven exploration of how habits and skills relate to career readiness.  
- **Career safety** — Forward-looking views on volatility, obsolescence risk, and stability.  
- **Employer decision support** — Structured, evidence-based observations with transparency and ethics by design.

Inputs are **consent-based**; outputs are framed strictly as **decision-support insights** for people and organisations, not final judgments.
